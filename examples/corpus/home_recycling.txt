Recycling at Home

Recycling reduces waste sent to landfills and saves raw materials. Paper, cardboard, glass, and many plastics can be processed into new products. Rinsing containers and sorting materials correctly helps recycling centers work efficiently.

Composting food scraps creates nutrient-rich soil for gardens. Reusing bags, bottles, and boxes cuts down on single-use trash. Small daily habits add up to meaningful environmental benefits over time.
