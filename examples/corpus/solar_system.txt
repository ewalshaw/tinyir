The Solar System

Our solar system has eight planets orbiting the Sun. Mercury is the closest planet and has extreme temperatures. Venus is covered in thick clouds. Earth is the only planet known to support life. Mars is called the red planet because of iron oxide on its surface.

Jupiter is the largest planet and has a famous storm called the Great Red Spot. Saturn is known for its bright rings made of ice and rock. Uranus and Neptune are icy giants far from the Sun. Many astronomers study these worlds to learn how planetary systems form.
