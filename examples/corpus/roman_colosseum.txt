The Roman Colosseum

The Colosseum in Rome is an ancient amphitheater built nearly two thousand years ago. It could hold tens of thousands of spectators for gladiator contests and public events. Arches and concrete construction made the structure both strong and innovative.

Today the Colosseum is a major historical landmark. Visitors walk through its corridors and imagine life in the Roman Empire. Preservation efforts protect the ruins from pollution, weather, and heavy tourism.
