Running a 5K Race

A 5K race is 3.1 miles and is a popular goal for new runners. Training plans often mix easy runs, interval workouts, and rest days. Consistency matters more than any single hard session.

Before race day, runners should hydrate, eat familiar foods, and sleep well. During the race, pacing early prevents burnout on the final mile. Crossing the finish line is a rewarding milestone for beginners and experienced athletes alike.
