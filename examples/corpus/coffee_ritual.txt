Morning Coffee Ritual

Many people start the day with a cup of coffee. Roasted beans are ground and brewed with hot water to extract flavor and caffeine. Espresso, drip coffee, and cold brew each taste different.

Baristas adjust grind size, water temperature, and brew time to balance bitterness and sweetness. A calm morning routine can make studying or commuting feel more focused. Coffee shops also serve as social meeting places in many neighborhoods.
