Intro to Jazz Music

Jazz began in the United States in the early twentieth century. Musicians often improvise melodies over chord changes, creating spontaneous performances. Swing, bebop, and cool jazz each brought new rhythms and harmonies.

Typical jazz groups include piano, bass, drums, and horns. Legendary artists such as Louis Armstrong and Ella Fitzgerald shaped the genre's history. Jazz influenced rock, hip hop, and modern popular music around the world.
